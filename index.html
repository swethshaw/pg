Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check which college I will get in NEET PG 2026?

You can use our NEET PG College Predictor to know your admission chances. Simply enter your All India Rank (AIR) and category to see the list of MD, MS, and DNB colleges you can get based on previous year MCC counselling cutoffs.

Is the NEET PG College Predictor accurate?

Our predictor is highly reliable as it uses official MCC allotment data from the previous year. While it provides strong historical indicators based on closing ranks, actual allotments depend on seat availability, candidate preferences, and current year counselling rules.

What is a safe rank for MD/MS in government colleges?

Safe ranks vary significantly by specialty and category. Clinical branches like MD Radio-Diagnosis or MD General Medicine typically require a top AIR (under 5,000 for general category), while non-clinical branches have lower cutoffs. Use the predictor to check specific specialties.

Does the NEET PG predictor include state counselling cutoffs?

This specific predictor focuses on the All India Quota (AIQ) and central counselling conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), which includes 50% AIQ seats, Deemed/Central Universities, AFMS, and DNB seats. It does not include state-specific quota counselling data.

Are all rounds of MCC counselling included in the predictions?

Yes, the predictions are based on comprehensive data covering all MCC counselling rounds, including Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, and the Stray Vacancy Round, to give you a complete picture of admission possibilities.

How is the closing rank determined in NEET PG counselling?

The closing rank is the highest (or last) All India Rank at which a candidate was allotted a specific seat (college, course, and category) during the MCC counselling process.
