What is the difference between NEET PG AIQ and State Quota?

When looking at the difference between AIQ and State Quota NEET PG, All India Quota NEET PG (AIQ) includes 50% of PG seats in government medical colleges plus 100% seats in central/deemed universities, managed through NEET PG AIQ counselling by MCC. NEET PG State Quota covers the remaining 50% of state government college seats, managed through NEET PG state quota counselling by respective state authorities. AIQ is open to candidates from any state provided they meet NEET PG AIQ eligibility, while State Quota typically requires NEET PG domicile in that state to meet NEET PG state quota eligibility. Understanding NEET PG AIQ vs State Quota is essential for navigating the counselling process.
